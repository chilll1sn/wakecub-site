Last updated: 2026-07-22

These terms cover Wake cub Premium, the subscription offered inside Wake cub (the “App”). Downloading and using the App also means you accept Apple’s Licensed Application End User License Agreement. Where this page and Apple’s standard terms conflict, this page governs the subscription specifics described here; Apple’s standard terms apply to everything else.

What the subscription includes

Wake cub Premium unlocks:

  • The camera-verified wake-up tasks: brushing, punching, squats, jogging and photo matching
  • Sending a friend a nudge that uses one of those camera tasks

The number of alarms you can set is not part of the subscription: the free tier is not limited to one.

Weekly, monthly and annual plans are available. The price that applies is the one shown in the App, provided by the App Store for your region at the time of purchase.

Free trial

The annual plan includes a 7-day free trial. Nothing is charged during the trial; when it ends, the subscription renews automatically at the price of the plan you selected unless you cancel before the trial is over.

Eligibility for the trial is determined by the App Store. If you have used a trial for the same item before, it may not be offered again — the terms and price shown on the purchase screen in the App are what apply.

Automatic renewal and cancellation

Subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each period unless you cancel at least 24 hours before that period ends. Payment is charged to your Apple ID account on confirmation of purchase, and renewals are charged within 24 hours before the current period ends.

You can manage or cancel a subscription at any time on your iPhone under Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions. After cancelling, access you have already paid for continues until the end of that period; it is not cut off immediately and is not refunded pro rata.

Refunds

All transactions are handled by Apple. Refund requests go to Apple via reportaproblem.apple.com; the developer of this App cannot issue refunds directly.

Payment and account

Every subscription purchase is processed by the Apple App Store. We never see or store your card or payment details. We use a third-party service, RevenueCat, to confirm your subscription status, solely to determine whether you can use the paid features.

What happens to alarms you have already set

A subscription ending does not alter alarms you have already configured — they ring with the task you originally set, so a subscription expiring overnight will not change the task waiting for you in the morning. Your subscription status only affects whether you can add or edit Premium tasks and extra alarms from then on.
